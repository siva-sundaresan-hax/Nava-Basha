Every day

One session, three blocks

New ground is only ever broken in the anchor. Satellites are chosen by the auction — highest decay debt wins — not by mood. Scripts get their own slot because they decay differently from speech.

15 min — AnchorDeep work on the day's language: lesson, phrase set, listening + shadowing.
10 min — Satellites2 × 5 min review drills in two other languages, picked by highest decay debt.
5 min — Script sprintReading + writing only. One script per day.

The week at a glance

  • Mon Hindi · Tue Telugu · Wed Spanish · Thu Malayalam · Fri German · Sat Tamil (formal)
  • Sunday reshapes: Boss Fight (10 min, weakest track — win +100, show up +25) → English lab → script sprint
  • Script sprint cycle: Tamil → Devanagari → Telugu → Malayalam → repeat
  • Points: 10 per study minute, split by block to each language

Eclipse days

Some days will be chaos, and life will not ask permission.

  • A declared 10-minute minimum (anchor only) keeps the streak alive at reduced points.
  • Declaring an eclipse costs nothing. Skipping silently is the only real loss. Rahu and Ketu approve.
