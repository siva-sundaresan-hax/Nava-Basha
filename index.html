Why it works

The game underneath

Six mechanics, all game theory — not gamification glitter. Each one solves a specific way language practice usually fails.

01

The Forgetting Curve is the adversary — minimax

Memory decays predictably. The Curve “attacks” where retention is weakest, so your optimal play is to minimise your maximum loss: study what’s closest to being forgotten, not what’s most fun.

02

The morning auction — mechanism design

Each language bids for the two satellite slots with its decay debt. Highest debts win. Honest self-reporting is the dominant strategy — inflating a bid only steals time from a language that needs it more.

03

The weekly stake — Ulysses contract

Every Sunday you stake 150 points on the coming week. Each completed day pays 30 — so a perfect 7-day week returns 210: your 150 back plus a 60-point completion bonus. A missed day forfeits its 30. You tie yourself to the mast while you’re strong, for the tired Tuesday evening ahead.

04

The Boss Fight — weakest-link test

Sunday, 10 minutes in the week’s weakest language. The adversary picks the battlefield, so the rational move all week is to shore up the weakest link. Win +100, show up +25.

05

The repeated game — cooperation bonus

A streak is a repeated game against your future self. Cooperate today and tomorrow’s player inherits momentum and a multiplier. If Prerna plays any track, shared days pay both of you +25%.

06

Reward cards — variable-ratio reinforcement

After every session, one card: a phrase worth knowing, a song, a scene, a piece of graha lore, occasionally a wild card. You never know which is coming — that unpredictability is what makes it stick.

Points economy

  • Study minute = 10 pts to that language (full session = 300, split by block)
  • Boss fight: win +100 · show up +25
  • Weekly stake: 150 in, 30 per completed day (perfect week = 210: stake back + 60 bonus)
  • Streak multiplier: ×1.1 at 7 days · ×1.25 at 30 days
  • Spend on: anchor override (200) · boss-fight postponement (300, once/month) · immersion day unlock (1,000)

First 90 days

What winning looks like

Not fluency in nine languages in 90 days. Placement, consistency, and one travel-ready track — with no track left behind.

  • All nine tracks placed by end of Week 1
  • Spanish at Level 2 — travel-ready — by Day 90
  • All four scripts readable at Level 1 — sign, menu, bus board
  • Tamil: one short formal text read end-to-end, unaided
  • 75+ sessions logged out of ~90
  • At least one Boss Fight won on every track
  • No track left at Level 0 after Day 30
  • Streak alive — eclipse days count, silent skips don’t
